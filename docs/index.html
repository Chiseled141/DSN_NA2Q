NA²Q Value Decomposition

GRU-based per-agent Q-networks with a VAE identity encoder and GAM mixer for interpretable credit assignment. Trained with epsilon-greedy exploration and curriculum learning over 8,395 episodes with tuned hyperparameters.

HiT-MAC Hierarchical A3C

Two-level hierarchy: a coordinator (Shapley-value critic, attention encoder) assigns targets every k=10 steps; executors track assigned targets with A3C. Phase 1 (executor) shown here — Phase 2 (coordinator) pending.
