NA²Q Performance

Demonstrates superior peak performance with rapid convergence. The attention mechanism enables efficient credit assignment and faster adaptation in cooperative sensor coordination.

HiT-MAC Performance

Shows stable, consistent learning through hierarchical structure. Requires more training samples but offers robust convergence for complex multi-agent coordination tasks.

Key Insight: NA²Q's exploration variance leads to higher peaks, while HiT-MAC prioritizes stability—both valid strategies depending on deployment requirements.
